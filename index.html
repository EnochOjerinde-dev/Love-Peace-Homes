Questions & Answers

What is independent living? +
It is a shared housing model where residents live together in a residential home, sharing common areas while maintaining personal responsibility for their daily lives.
Can I live here with my children? +
Yes. We have dedicated options for women with children, focused on a safe and child-friendly atmosphere.
Is there a requirement for sobriety? +
Yes. We maintain a strict substance-free environment to ensure the safety and peace of all residents.
