Questions & Answers

What is independent living?
A shared housing model where residents live together in a residential home, sharing common areas while maintaining personal responsibility for their growth and stability.
Can I live here with my children?
Yes. We have dedicated options for women and women with children, focused on a safe, child-friendly atmosphere.
Is there a requirement for sobriety?
Yes. We maintain a strict substance-free environment to ensure the total safety and peace of all residents.
